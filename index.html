I'm a product thinker who'd rather be right than impressive.

📍Based in West Orange, NJ, open to relocating. 🎓Recent grad of NYU Stern (Business, Technology & Entrepreneurship). Currently interviewing for APM and product strategy roles.

A few things I keep coming back to when I build:

  1. 01 Talk to the person before you build for them. Most of the work below started with interviews, not assumptions — 50+ caregiver conversations before a line of Tafia's escalation logic got written.
  2. 02 A claim you can't source is just a guess. Every number on this site is one I can walk you through — where it came from, what it doesn't prove, and what I'd want to test next.
  3. 03 Build the counterargument before someone else does. Whether it's a product decision or a legal memo, I try to argue against myself first — it's usually where the real risk is hiding.
